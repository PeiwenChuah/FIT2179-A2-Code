Section 1

National Healthcare Overview

Two decades of sustained growth have transformed Malaysia's health system, yet structural gaps persist across states and across urban and rural divides.

Section 3

Healthcare Workforce

Malaysia's healthcare workforce has grown rapidly, but growth has not been uniform. Geographic concentration of professionals in urban centres leaves rural communities under-served.

Total Staff Count, 2014 to 2022

Stacked count by profession type · Annual

Key Insight

Total healthcare staff grew nearly 50%

in eight years across all professions.

Pharmacists saw the fastest growth,

driven by expanded retail and hospital

pharmacy services after 2016.

Rates per 10,000 Population, 1990 to 2023

Professional density over time · Annual

Key Insight

Doctor rate grew steadily from 4 to 18

per 10,000 over three decades.

Pharmacist density surged post-2010

as community pharmacy roles expanded.

Nurse numbers show high variance,

likely from shifting reporting definitions.

W.P. Kuala Lumpur has over 60 doctors

most under-served states in the country.

Section 5

Healthcare Finance

Malaysia spends RM 89.8 billion on health annually. Tracing how funds flow from source to function exposes who bears the cost and where spending is most concentrated.

Flow of Health Funds — RM 89,827 Million

Key Insight

Out-of-pocket spending at RM 34.8 billion

is nearly as large as the entire MOH budget.

This places a heavy burden on households,

particularly those without insurance cover.
